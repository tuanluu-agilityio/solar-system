## Decisions
- DECISION: `UiKey = keyof typeof EN` where the English table is declared `as const`; the Vietnamese table is typed `Record<UiKey, string>`, so adding a key (e.g. Task 24's `unit.*`) to EN makes TypeScript demand it in VI.
- DECISION: Sentences are counted by splitting on `.`/`!`/`?` followed by whitespace; descriptions avoid abbreviations so the count is reliable. Decimals ("2.2", "2,2") are not split.
- DECISION: Prose avoids exact moon counts for planets whose counts change often (Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune); the panel's `field.moons` comes from body data. Pluto's five moons are named.
- DECISION: `asteroid-belt` kindLabel is "Region of the Solar System" / "Vùng trong Hệ Mặt Trời" (not a `BodyKind`). The Moon's kindLabel is "Natural satellite" / "Vệ tinh tự nhiên"; `kind.moon` is the shorter "Moon" / "Vệ tinh".
- DECISION: The 2.2–3.2 AU belt range comes from the plan brief (the NASA asteroid facts page gives no AU range); the Ceres 25% share comes from the NASA Ceres page.
- DECISION: The extra tests cover: Pluto content mentions 2006 and IAU in both languages; the belt mentions 2.2, 3.2, AU, Ceres and the Moon; the credits strings match the required attribution text exactly (en); `tour.progress` has both placeholders.
