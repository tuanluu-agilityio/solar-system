## Summary

- `src/main.ts`: after `rig.flyTo` resolves, if `tierSettings(quality).textureSet === 'high'` (not for the asteroid belt), `loadHighTextures(id)` then `upgradeTextures`; fire-and-forget, so `select` is not delayed.

## Decisions
- DECISION: the e2e "no `textures/high/` requests" on mobile excludes `sky_*`: medium tier (mobile with more than 4 cores) loads `sky_4096` from the `high` set at boot, so only body maps are asserted.
- DECISION: the Moon, Venus and Saturn go through the same path; Venus swaps the first real key of `venus_atmosphere`, `venus_surface`. The sun is skipped (its own shader).
- DECISION: the desktop e2e uses a 90 s poll (150 s test timeout): under 3 parallel software-GL projects the flight alone can take more than 30 s.
- DECISION: if the high Saturn ring arrives while the low one was a plain band, it is switched to the textured ring.

- DECISION (fix round 1): the high-res load runs only if, after `flyTo` resolves, `selected === id`, `view === 'focus'` and `cameraTargetId() === id` (a superseded flight resolves early). The load chain has a no-op `.catch`.
- DECISION (fix round 1): `loadHighTextures` drops its cache entry when every texture was a fallback, so a transient failure is retried on the next focus.
