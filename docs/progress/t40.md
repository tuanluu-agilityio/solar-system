# T40: Link to Deep Space Odyssey

## Status

Implemented; prototype approved by the user on 2026-11-14.

## Summary

Links to the author's companion project (https://deep-space-odyssey.netlify.app/, opens in a new
tab) in three places: under the title (`src/ui/controlBar.ts`, hidden while a body is selected
and during a tour), as the last chip of the body navigator (`src/ui/bodyNav.ts`) and in the
credits dialog (`src/ui/credits.ts`, `DEEP_SPACE_URL`). Strings `beyond.link` and `beyond.hint`
in vi/en/ja/de.
