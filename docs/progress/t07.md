## Decisions
- DECISION: The localized-name check is case-insensitive, because the English names are "The Sun" / "The Moon" and read naturally mid-sentence as "the Sun" / "the Moon".
- DECISION: Extra TTS-friendliness tests: no markdown characters, emoji, newlines, or symbols such as `°`, `%`, `²`, `₂`, `&`; units are spelled out ("degrees Celsius", "độ C", "percent", "phần trăm", "kilômét", "xentimét", "khí cacbonic").
- DECISION: The test also checks each text ends with `.`, `!` or `?`, and that intro/outro focus `overview` while body stops focus themselves.
- DECISION: English avoids decimal points inside numbers ("one point four million") and Vietnamese avoids thousand-separator dots ("12 nghìn 800"), so `.` only ever ends a sentence for subtitle splitting.
- DECISION: Values are rounded for speech where the info panel shows exact data (e.g. Earth "nearly 12,800 km" in vi, Uranus orbit "about 84 years" from 30,589 days, Neptune "about 4.5 billion km"). Moon counts (115, 293) are quoted from `bodies.ts` for Jupiter and Saturn only.
